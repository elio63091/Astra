1. Information We May Process

Depending on which features are enabled and how Astra is configured, the bot may process:

  • Discord account information: Discord user IDs, usernames, display names, and server, channel, or message IDs needed to operate the bot.
  • Message and conversation content: Messages sent to Astra, relevant surrounding chat context, prompts, and the bot's responses.
  • Memory information: Details the bot saves to personalize future conversations, such as user-provided interests, preferences, projects, or goals.
  • Commands and settings: Commands used, channel preferences, bot configuration, and administrator settings.
  • Technical information: Error reports and basic operational logs used to maintain and troubleshoot the service.

Astra should not ask you for passwords, payment card details, or other highly sensitive information. Please do not send such information to the bot.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Generate replies and maintain conversational context.
  • Provide per-user memory and personalization features.
  • Operate commands and apply server or channel settings.
  • Prevent spam, abuse, and technical problems.
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the bot's reliability.

3. AI Providers and Other Services

To generate AI responses, Astra may send message content and relevant context to the third-party AI service configured by its operator. The provider processes that information under its own privacy policy and terms.

Discord also processes information when you use its platform. Astra does not control Discord's data practices or those of any third-party provider.

Before publishing: Identify the AI provider and any database or hosting services actually used by Astra, and link to their current privacy policies where appropriate.

4. Memory, Storage, and Retention

If memory features are enabled, Astra may store information associated with your Discord user ID so it can use relevant details in later conversations. Memory is intended to be separated by user and should not be disclosed to other users.

Information may remain stored until it is deleted, the relevant feature is reset, or the operator removes it. Technical backups or logs may persist for a limited period depending on the services used.

5. Your Choices and Deletion Requests

If supported by the version of Astra in use, you can use:

  • /memory to view the memories associated with your account.
  • /forget to delete a saved memory or request that remembered information be removed.
  • /reset to clear your personal conversation memory.

These commands may not delete records held independently by Discord, an AI provider, or infrastructure providers. For other privacy requests, contact the bot operator using the contact details below.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not intend to sell personal information. Information may be processed by the service providers needed to operate Astra, or disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the bot, its users, or others from abuse or security threats.

Private memory associated with one user should not be shown to another user. However, avoid sharing confidential information with any AI chatbot.

7. Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect stored information and access to the bot's systems. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Never share bot credentials, tokens, or private account information in Discord conversations.

8. Children and Age Requirements

Astra is provided through Discord and is subject to Discord's age requirements and applicable law. The bot is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable legal requirements.
